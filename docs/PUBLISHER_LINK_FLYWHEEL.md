# Michigan Outdoors Now Publisher Link Flywheel

## Goal

Create legitimate external distribution that sends qualified Michigan outdoor users into Michigan Outdoors Now while earning transparent citations to the planner and Chris Izworski.

This is an authority and referral system, not a new search-page family.

## Flywheel

1. A Michigan publisher installs the free widget with visible attribution.
2. The publisher page contains a static crawlable link to Michigan Outdoors Now.
3. A visitor opens the planner through a tagged referral link.
4. Publisher source and campaign are sanitized and stored for the browser session only.
5. Existing product events inherit that referral attribution.
6. We can identify which publishers produce planner starts, completed plans, day builds, and directions.
7. Successful placements become proof for the next publisher pitch.

## Search boundary

The publisher kit is deliberately noindex, follow.

The JavaScript widget endpoint is deliberately noindex, nofollow, noarchive.

The publisher system does not:
- create a new crawlable intent family;
- add publisher surfaces to the sitemap;
- change current title, H1, canonical, or protected location-intent experiments;
- alter Trail Truth search-family expansion gates.

The backlink comes from the publisher's own page through the static fallback anchor included in the installation snippet.

## Attribution and privacy

Publisher links use:
- utm_source=<publisher label>
- utm_medium=referral
- utm_campaign=michigan_outdoors_now_embed or michigan_outdoors_now_attribution

Only sanitized source/campaign labels are retained in sessionStorage. No exact coordinates, device location, free-text search, or publisher-page visitor data is collected by the widget.

The widget itself does not call Michigan Outdoors Now analytics while it is embedded on another site. Measurement begins after the visitor chooses to open Michigan Outdoors Now.

## 30-day traction targets

These are operating targets, not guaranteed outcomes.

- 5 legitimate new referring domains
- 3 live publisher widget placements
- 150 qualified publisher-referred landings
- 30 publisher-referred planner starts
- 15 publisher-referred completed plans or day builds
- 10 publisher-referred directions opens
- 2 external citations that name Chris Izworski as the tool creator

## Placement priorities

Prioritize pages where the widget solves an immediate visitor problem.

### Tier 1 — regional destination publishers
- convention and visitors bureaus
- chambers with visitor sections
- county and municipal tourism resources
- regional outdoor/travel publishers

### Tier 2 — lodging and trip-planning businesses
- cabin and cottage operators
- campgrounds and RV parks
- independent hotels and resorts
- guide/outfitter resource pages

### Tier 3 — community distribution
- local news/weather resource pages
- university outdoor recreation pages
- trail and recreation nonprofits
- Michigan outdoor bloggers with durable resource pages

Avoid low-quality directories, paid-link networks, reciprocal-link schemes, mass guest-post networks, and unrelated placements.

## Publisher pitch

Lead with utility, not SEO:

> Give your visitors a free way to answer "what should I do outside today?" from your part of Michigan. The widget is lightweight, requires no account, can open a local starting-point planner, and keeps your visitor on a useful decision path. There is no affiliate requirement or booking handoff.

## Qualification standard

A placement is worth pursuing when all are true:
- the host has a real Michigan audience;
- the page has durable visitor utility;
- the link is editorially relevant;
- attribution is visible;
- the publisher is not being compensated for a followed link;
- the widget improves the host page rather than merely adding a backlink.

## Next expansion

Do not create more publisher widgets merely to create links.

First measure which publisher categories produce real planning behavior. Expand the distribution kit only when evidence shows a useful host-page use case, such as:
- a city-specific starting point;
- a trail/hiking-specific module;
- a seasonal opportunity module;
- a destination-specific trip-planning module.

The product-value signal should decide the next distribution surface.
